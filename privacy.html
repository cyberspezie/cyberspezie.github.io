who

This site is the portfolio of cyberspezie (), Milan, Italy, who is responsible for it (the “data controller” under the GDPR). Contact: .

in short

This site uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. It stores nothing in your browser and has no forms. Fonts, images, videos and code are all served by the site itself.

hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. (USA). As on every GitHub Pages site, GitHub logs the IP address of each visitor for security purposes. See the GitHub privacy statement ↗.

embedded works

  • cablato: the film is played by Vimeo (Vimeo.com, Inc., USA) in its “do not track” mode, which turns off Vimeo’s tracking and statistics. When you open this work your browser connects to Vimeo, which receives your IP address and may set cookies strictly needed for the player and its security. See the Vimeo privacy policy ↗.
  • nostalgic path: the two live channels run on cables.gl (undefined development UG, Germany) and load only when you press “run live”; until then nothing is sent to cables.gl. Once loaded, cables.gl receives your IP address. See the cables.gl imprint and privacy ↗.
  • costellazioni: the sketch runs from this site; no third party is involved.

The embedded works are shown on the basis of a legitimate interest in presenting the artistic work (art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

links

Links to other sites (Instagram, LinkedIn, GitHub, Google Drive, Vimeo, cables.gl, the p5.js editor) take you to those sites, whose own privacy policies apply.

email

If you write to me, I use your address and your message only to reply, and keep them only as long as our conversation needs (art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). They are never shared with anyone.

your rights

Under the GDPR (arts. 15–22) you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, and to restrict or object to its processing, by writing to the address above. Wherever you live in the EU or EEA, you can also lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of your country (art. 77 GDPR): the list of national authorities ↗ is kept by the European Data Protection Board. The authority responsible for this site is the Italian Data Protection Authority.
